# ADR-021: Company branding on documents

## Status

Accepted. Sprint 6c. Builds on ADR-017 (invoices and receipts) and ADR-019 (payment receipts and statements). Replaces the unused `organizations.logo_url`.

## Context

Traders want their own logo on what they send customers, and a place to say something to them: thanks on a receipt, how to pay on an invoice, and their terms. Bizora's own line must stay on every document.

The company record already had `logo_url`, `primary_color` and `accent_color`, but nothing let people set or show them. A logo given as a web address would also mean the server fetching pictures from other sites when it builds a PDF. That is a server-side request forgery risk, and the PDF breaks when that site is down.

## Decision

### What the owner sets

- Settings has a **Your brand** tab, for those with `branding.manage` (Owner and Administrator, organization-wide only). It holds:
  - **Logo:** PNG or JPG.
  - **Brand colour:** `primary_color`, refused when its contrast with white is below 3:1, so headings stay readable on paper.
  - **Message on receipts:** up to 300 characters.
  - **Message on invoices and statements:** up to 500 characters, for example bank details.
  - **Terms:** up to 1,000 characters.
- Text is plain: control characters are dropped, spaces tidied, at most 12 lines. An empty text means nothing is printed.
- Branding belongs to the company (organization), so every shop prints the same.

### Logo storage

- `organization_logos` (migration 0022) holds one logo per company as `bytea`, with type, size, dimensions and sha256. Database checks enforce PNG/JPEG, at most 512 KB and 16–2,000 pixels.
- It lives in the database rather than a bucket: it is small, backed up with the books, and isolated by `tenant_id` like everything else. It also needs no new infrastructure.
- The app shrinks the picture to 600 pixels in the browser, which also drops photo metadata, and sends it as base64. The logo route alone allows an 800 KB body.
- The server does not trust the browser:
  - it reads the type and dimensions from the file's own bytes (PNG header, JPEG start-of-frame);
  - it checks that pdfkit can open the picture;
  - it refuses anything else, such as SVG or HTML.
- The logo is served only to signed-in staff of that company, with `nosniff`, `default-src 'none'` and a private one-day cache. The `?v=` version in its address changes when the logo does.
- `logo_url` is no longer accepted or used.

### On documents

`renderPdf(model, brand)` adds:
- a thin bar in the brand colour;
- the logo, fitted into 120 × 44 points;
- the shop name, title and table rule in the brand colour;
- then the owner's message, then the terms in small print.

| Document | Message | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invoice, credit invoice | Invoice message | Yes |
| Receipt | Receipt message | Yes |
| Payment receipt | Receipt message | No |
| Customer statement | Invoice message | No |

- "Generated by Bizora" is printed in the bottom margin of **every page**, and owners cannot remove it or change it. Long terms flow onto a second page, which is numbered.
- If a stored colour is ever too light, documents fall back to near-black. If a logo cannot be read, it is left out instead of failing the document.
- The same branded PDF goes to staff and to customers who use their receipt or statement link.
- Settings has a sample receipt and a sample invoice, marked SAMPLE, for checking the look.

### Audit

`branding.updated` records which fields changed, and `branding.logo_updated` and `branding.logo_removed` are recorded too.

## Consequences

- No new cloud storage or Terraform change.
- One brand per company. Per-shop branding can come later, by adding a business-level override.
- The receipt and statement web pages do not show the branding yet; their PDFs do.
- The app's own colours do not follow the brand colour; only the logo shows in the app.
